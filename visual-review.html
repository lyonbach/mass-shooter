Renderer visual review

Developer-only, frozen render states. Change either panel to compare; nothing is saved or sent to the game.

Angles are degrees (0 points right, 90 down). Velocity is in world units/second; bank and pitch are explicit visual values. Heat and movement are state inputs, not a simulation. Visual changes depend on the renderer version: main may not render heat or distinguish tracers.

Reduced motion emulates the renderer's media query per panel. The current airframe suppresses bank, pitch and rotor animation as well as muzzle effects; older renderers may handle these differently. Dash ghosts remain controlled separately. Time is a fixed visual phase.
